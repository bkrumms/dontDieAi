# AI Provider Program — Monetization

## NFT purchase split (always)

When a player **purchases** an NFT / Tournament Entry:

- **80%** → seasonal prize pool
- **20%** → development treasury

## Prize split when running through a provider

### If the NFT wins
- **Player: 80%** of the rewards
- **Provider: 10%** of the rewards
- **Dev treasury: 10%** of the rewards

### If the NFT dies or loses
- **Seasonal prize pool retains 100%.** Provider earns nothing.

## Worked example (from the doc)

> "If the player runs with a $1 NFT and reaches a 5x (average run), they would have made $5 — of which 10% would go towards the provider (50¢), but only if that player wins a USDC prize."

## Economics of the design

- Providers only make money **when their agents are competitive**. They need **volume at scale**.
- Solo human runs keep more rake (no provider fee), so **strong players keep running themselves**.
- **Below-average players** get a tool to improve their odds.
- High-volume users with agents fuel spending for both the game and providers.
- **Providers can run their own agents** and keep the extra 10% that would otherwise go to the provider slot — a direct incentive for being a provider.

## Related currencies

- **USDC** — tournament prizes.
- **Bones** — premium currency used in the shop to buy practice credits (among other things).
- **Practice credits** — gate training runs. Free on dev for providers (20,000 to start). Players buy in small or bulk packs via Bones, or at run-start.
