# Biomes

On the map, forks in the road lead to one of **3 Biomes**. Each biome has thematic enemies, matching trinkets, and **at least 2 of the 3 post-battle Side options will match the biome**. Pick the biome that matches the Sides you need.

## La Volcano 🌋

- Firey hellscape with high-damage enemies.
- Themes: **powerful attacks, strength, and bleed modifiers**.

## Ice Cave ❄️

- Frozen, haunted cave; drawn-out battles of endurance.
- Themes: **powerful blocks, armor, and freeze modifiers**.

## Toxic Swamp ☠️

- Deadly world where poison seeps into your veins — "death is inevitable if you can't end the battle".
- Themes: **poison, debuff management, and re-healths**.
