# Winning Side Placement Guide

Data from 33 clearing runs. Shows which die each side performs best on.
"Strong" = 60%+ of appearances on one die type.

## Attack Die (Atk4 x2 / AtkAll4 / Atk6Bleed)

**Best picks (in order):**
- Attack 14, Gain 2 Strength (11x) — strength stacking, best here
- (e) Exhaust all Basic Attack (9x) — consistency, STRONG fit
- Attack 28 (7x) — raw damage
- Increase Damage by 2x next turn (6x) — STRONG fit here
- Attack All 10 (6x) — AoE
- (p) Heal 8 at end of battle (7x) — sustain
- Attack 16, Duplicate this Side (12x) — great everywhere
- Block 24 if you have no block (5x) — STRONG fit, must reorder to slot 1
- (p) Gain 4 Strength at end of turn (5x) — compounding

## Buff Die (Str / Armor / Poison3 / Atk4)

**Best picks (in order):**
- Attack 16, Duplicate this Side (25x) — #1 pick by far, BEST die for it
- (e) Attack 16, Permanently +2 (12x) — scaling damage
- Echo Dagger (10x) — STRONG fit, damage multiplier
- Attack 6, 2 times (7x) — multi-hit + strength synergy
- Attack 12, Freeze 3 (7x) — freeze combo
- (e) Poison all 2, 3 times (7x) — AoE poison
- Attack 16, Bleed 2, Freeze 2 (6x) — debuff applier
- Attack 6 per Exhausted Side (5x) — scales with exhausts
- (e) Freeze all 3, Bleed 4, Poison 5 (5x) — AoE debuff

## Mixed Die (Atk4 x2 / Blk6 x2) → Hybrid Die

The mixed die is the most flexible die and becomes a hybrid that fills
whatever the build needs. It gets upgrades first (must have >4 sides
before any starters can be burned), then Block 6 starters get burned at
campfires/Bub's/mysteries to improve consistency. Of 33 clears, 32 had
this die's Block 6 starters burned.

**Best picks (in order):**
- Attack 16, Duplicate this Side (10x) — damage carry
- Attack 10 if Freezing +20 (10x) — conditional (needs freeze source)
- Attack 28 (7x) — raw damage
- Block 8, Gain 1 Armor (7x) — defense when build needs it
- (p) Heal 2 at end of each turn (6x) — sustain fill
- Attack 12, Freeze 3 (5x) — freeze combo
- (p) Heal 8 at end of battle (5x) — sustain fill
- Block 4, remove debuff, exhaust curses (5x) — utility
- (e) Block 40 (5x) — burst block
- (e) Heal = unblocked Damage (5x) — exhaust heal

This die fills gaps: if the build lacks healing, it gets heal sides. If
the build lacks damage, it gets attack sides. Block 6 starters are the
#1 burn target on this die once it has upgrades.

## Defense Die (Blk6 x3 / BlkFreezeAll)

**Best picks (in order):**
- Block 12 at end of each turn (11x) — STRONG #1 defense pick, passive
- Block 8, Gain 1 Armor (8x) — armor stacking
- Block 3 times the current turn (7x) — STRONG, synergy with armor
- (e) Block 40 (6x) — burst block
- (e) Block 2, Permanently +4 (6x) — scaling block
- (e) Heal = unblocked Damage (6x) — exhaust heal, good here
- (p) Any attack gives 3 Block (6x) — STRONG passive
- Negate Damage 2 times (5x) — damage prevention
- (e) Poison all 2, 3 times (5x) — exhaust attack, OK here
- (e) Decrease Baddie Strength 15 (5x) — STRONG exhaust debuff
- Attack equal to Block (5x) — STRONG, only with heavy block elsewhere
- Block 4, remove debuff, exhaust curses (5x) — utility

## Sides to AVOID on Defense Die
- Attack 10 if Freezing +20 — non-exhaust attack, wastes block rolls
- Attack 16, Duplicate this Side — non-exhaust attack
- Any non-exhaust attack side — competes with block rolls

## Universal Good Sides (work on multiple dice)
- Attack 16, Duplicate this Side — buff > attack > mixed (never defense)
- Attack 28 — attack or buff die
- (e) Attack 16, Permanently +2 — buff > attack
- (p) Heal 8 at end of battle — any die
- Increase Damage by 1x (33% exhaust) — any die

## Bub Shop Placement Rules
When buying a side at Bub's, place it on the die where it appeared most
in winning runs:
- Echo Dagger → buff die (10/12 clears)
- Block 12/turn → defense die (11/17)
- Exhaust all Basic Attack → attack die (9/14)
- Attack 14 + Str → attack die (11/24)
- Block 8 + Armor → defense die (8/17)
