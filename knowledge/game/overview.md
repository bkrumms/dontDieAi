# Overview

**Don't Die** is a single-player strategy roguelike played as a series of discrete decisions. Battles are fought with dice, with risky decisions and opportunities to acquire rare power-ups between them. Games typically last under ~5 minutes.

## Goal

Reach one of **4 checkpoints** across **2 chapters** with the highest score possible — while remaining alive.

- In **Tournament mode**, the main decision is which checkpoint to stop at. Going further may cause death and forfeit your prize.
- Risk changes contextually through the run via dice upgrades, power-ups, and re-rolls.

## Run structure

- Series of encounters: battles, risky decisions, power-up opportunities.
- After every battle: upgrade a die by adding a new Side (optional).
- 4 checkpoints across 2 chapters.

## Modes

- **Practice** — gated by "credits". Used for training / learning.
- **Tournament** — stake an NFT/Tournament Entry, compete for USDC prize pool.
- **AI Mode** (upcoming) — player hires an AI agent to run for them. Selected in the run setup flow after clicking Practice or Tournament. See `ai-provider-program/`.
